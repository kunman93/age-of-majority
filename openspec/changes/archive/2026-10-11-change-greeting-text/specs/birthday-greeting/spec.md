# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Greets visitors of the birthday card with a heading that names the occasion: reaching the age of majority.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Home page shows the age of majority greeting
The home page SHALL show a heading with the exact text "Happy Age of Majority!".

#### Scenario: Visitor opens the home page
- **WHEN** a visitor opens the home page
- **THEN** the visitor sees the heading "Happy Age of Majority!"
- **AND** the heading "Happy 18th Birthday!" is not shown
